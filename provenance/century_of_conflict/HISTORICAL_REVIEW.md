# Century of Conflict — historical review and research queue

22 October 2026 · scoped start references, not a complete operational census

The 36 new entries use an authored **15 December** Julian checkpoint after the year's described developments. The retained **1415 entry opens on 11 August**, before departure. These cutoffs select what the new world may reference; they are not dates assigned to every event, and they supply no hour. Existing campaigns keep their committed state.

`historical_reference` identifies an attributed context claim, not automatic proof. `research_inference` identifies a practical or causal application. `authored_policy` identifies deliberate setup choices. The local delivery is explicit fiction. In all four cases the original source and the campaign's subsequent actual state remain distinct.

The political sequence is a basis for selecting a start. It is not a complete reconstruction of every polity, town, family or individual's situation. The 99 person rows across the 37 snapshots repeat individuals across dates; they are not 99 distinct historical biographies.

## Research queue and milestones

Each item is an evidence deliverable. Unknown values must not be filled with game balance numbers, a modern average, a treaty claim or an invented convenient default.

| Priority | Work | Required output before operational use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 · delivered | Distinct entry identity, civil cutoff, scoped context and sources for all 37 starts | Exact dated snapshot/hash; no nearby-year substitution; preserved primary years and 1415 bytes |
| P0 · delivered | Reviewed independent character creation and optional local play | Explicit fiction/consent, finite entrusted object, existing Core effects, durable retry and actor-safe projection |
| P1 | One researched locality per entry, beginning with the ten primary starts | Dated gazetteer with contemporaneous/modern names, location uncertainty, route evidence and effective control separate from legal claims |
| P1 | Titles, people and institutional authority | Attested tenure intervals, regents/delegates, competing claims, kinship sources, explicit capacity/eligibility, no automatic personal allegiance or appointment |
| P1 | Local military and economic accounts | Muster attendance and service dates; actual stores, custody, debt, tax rights and units; verified opening balances only for the selected household/institution |
| P1 | Information travel and access | Dated letters, messengers, routes, receipt uncertainty and who could legitimately know a report at the cutoff |
| P2 | Population, epidemic and settlement change | Local series with geographic denominators, evidence dates, ranges and method; distinguish mortality, migration and fiscal/register coverage; no invented ethnic census |
| P2 | Cultural, religious and ethnic archive | Attestations and competing labels, endonyms/exonyms, localized practice and language, uncertain formation/decline ranges; no universal single founding/extinction date for a living identity |
| P2 | Growth and contraction | Measurements with consistent territory, dates, units and uncertainty; separate population/area/adherence indicators and document changes in classification |
| P2 | Conditional historical development | Replace generic research questions with event-specific actual prerequisites, competing intentions and feasible alternatives. Historical dates remain reference windows, not automatic execution triggers. |
| P3 | Wider world and post-1453 continuation | Expand evidence coverage while retaining the existing shared owners. Continued outcomes arise from actual decisions, capacity and information; no forced endpoint or reset. |
| Every stage | Rights and provenance review | Preserve archive hashes/paths and source-specific permissions. Promote only compatible original transformations or independently verified facts; do not redistribute the proprietary source collections. |

The existing Historical Grand Archive is a separate, mixed-rights research collection. This integration neither rebuilds it nor certifies all of its entities. Its private preservation layer and imported game values must remain distinct from this scoped historical-reference layer and from any new campaign's authoritative state.
