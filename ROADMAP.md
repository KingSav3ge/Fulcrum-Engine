## 1. Resolve one consequential action

Choose a supported operation with observable effects, such as an authorized equipment transfer. Implement a linked flow from exact player intent through authorization, observed reads, allocation and atomic effects to an outcome and durable receipt. An unavailable rule or missing fact must remain explicit. Do not turn a declaration into success by narration alone.

Completion evidence: real state changes follow the selected rule; invalid attempts leave no partial effects; retry returns the original accepted result without repeating effects; the app distinguishes saved intent, current status and completed outcome.

## 2. Certify dependencies across engines

Extend observation to actual records, capacities, resources, permissions, prerequisites and decisions used by every supported handler. Distinguish list reads from mutations and include failed branches, revocation, dissolution and competing claims. Manually supplied footprints or one successful example are not complete certification.

Completion evidence: relevant permutations and simultaneous frontiers agree where operations are independent; conflicts preserve unresolved intent; every claimed supported operation has named coverage and disclosed limits. Reuse the same owners and ledgers across genres.

## 3. Add durable phases and elapsed processes

Implement continuation identities, per-phase receipts, restart recovery and restore fencing. Keep storage revision, causal order, elapsed time, civil date and information times separate. Process supported spans and capacity reservations without replaying their booking or inventing an opening hour.

Completion evidence: interruption, restart, retry and restore do not duplicate resources, payments, work or knowledge. Conserved stock and processed spans reconcile at every phase boundary.

## 4. Connect movement, observation and dialogue

Implement the C2 adapters against the shared action cycle. Ordinary supported movement needs no artificial roll. A repeated receipt lookup causes no new search, while a new physical search needs supported time and preserves retained uncertainty. Speech, testimony, offers, acceptance and obligations retain distinct meanings and authority.

Completion evidence: the app presents only legitimate character knowledge and committed effects; narration cannot supply hidden validation facts or create arbitrary world changes. Setup backgrounds remain testimony until supported establishment occurs.

## 5. Advance institutions within one world
