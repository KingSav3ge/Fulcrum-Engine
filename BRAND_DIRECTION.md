# Product naming direction

29 October 2026. The proposed naming architecture is:

- **T!DEMARCH Studios** — developer and publisher.
- **WorldWard** — player-facing platform; **WORLDWARD** may be used for a cinematic title.
- **Fulcrum Engine** — underlying simulation technology.
- **IRGS** — internal development initiative and existing technical identifiers.

Preferred credit: “WorldWard — Developed by T!DEMARCH Studios. Powered by Fulcrum Engine.”
These names are the leading direction subject to commercial clearance; the
reported overlaps have not been independently verified by this record. Catalog
and Atlas naming remains undecided. Existing repositories, compatibility schemas,
content identities and historical source attributions retain their current names.
