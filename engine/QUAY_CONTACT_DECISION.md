# Selected quay contact decision

A4-ENG-01/03, non-Rise, bounded authored fiction. The new opt-in opening
`quay-contact-decision-v1` is separately reviewed in the existing mixed
`historical-quay` world-draft profile and in the normal starting options. The
existing `quay-contact-v1`, shared-intent API and communication APIs retain
their established behavior. Continuing saves keep their saved scene rules;
they are not silently migrated to the new variant.

The reviewed fictional worker independently declines requests to carry the
sealed dispatch. An explicit selected `request-dispatch-help` attempt requires
the controlled courier and the actual non-player worker to be co-located at the
fictional quay, the selected map and model, and no conflicting actor commitment.
The option comes from the received map and does not test hidden current presence,
possessions or authorization. Current presence and non-player control are
checked only at execution; missing prerequisites reject atomically with a generic
error. A request is not consent or transfer, and ordinary unstructured speech
does not select this decision.

The Core records the request for the worker and the worker's refusal as
separate actor-scoped testimony, spends 15 explicitly authored scene seconds and
returns the refusal in the existing immutable intent result. No stock, custody,
obligation, payment, authority or civil calendar is changed. The courier can
continue to the desk and deliver the actual dispatch under the pre-existing
reviewed instructions. Causal sequence and the 15-second fictional elapsed model
are not a historical opening hour or calendar advance. This fixed refusal is an
explicit selected policy, not a general model of workers or NPC preferences.
The refusal answers the request; `completed_successfully` denotes that the
conversation completed, not agreement to carry the dispatch.

The new opening requires a new human individual at the reviewed 1415 snapshot;
the draft validates those same bounded inputs. General NPC/institution behavior,
other campaign contexts, broader drafting and creator host persistence remain
open. The [draft host contract](DRAFT_HOST_ATOMIC_CONTRACT.md) is still design
only; this addition does not establish a Creator playtest workflow.
