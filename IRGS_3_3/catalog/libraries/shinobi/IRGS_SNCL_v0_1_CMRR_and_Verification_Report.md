## CMRR disposition

- **Retain:** every distinct source R0.3 §6.4 starter effect and §12.1 sample unit block; source native gear, care, mission procedure references.
- **Merge by reference:** Core actors/resources/time/organizations/work/medical/information; exact SCL `@0.2` shared catalogue handles under v1.0 release gate.
- **Specialize:** 31 new scene-scale Shinobi candidate techniques, 11 mission category templates, 12 institutional templates and service templates.
- **Hold:** physical summon/clone/pupperty/body stats, bloodline details, village-scale production, permanent seals, undefined explosive tool profiles and historic archives until complete contracts and source recovery.
- **Account for exclusion:** Combat/economy/Core action procedures not copied into a second rules engine. Historical hundreds of techniques are not represented as recovered.
- **Migration:** none; no automatic insertion of objects, skills, organization assets, hidden facts or adjustments to existing campaigns.

## Calibration/open issues

1. Validate Chakra resource efficiency, Overclock distribution, low-rank jutsu and use rates in actual scenes.
2. Test new Genjutsu agency, sensory perception and disruption against existing NPCs under non-scripted conditions.
3. Compare technique use and non-chakra labor for services, infrastructure and logistics; do not invent liters/hour, visits/day or manufactured item counts.
4. Recover historical SSN compendia before naming bloodlines, summoning contracts, setting-specific jutsu or mythic mechanisms as canon.
5. Bind case-specific equipment physical modes, price/access and material needs to valid selected SCL references where possible.
6. Test simultaneous effects, scale transitions, reports and institutional autonomy through live GM sessions.
7. Check the C-rank proposal `Water Slick`: it consumes existing water, and does not create fluid; a physical quantity profile is needed for precise volume/coverage beyond its limited scene effect.
8. Check the B-rank proposal `Chakra Anchorage`: no universal force threshold is supplied. It should not decide structure-scale loads or heavy vehicle immobilization without a load profile.
