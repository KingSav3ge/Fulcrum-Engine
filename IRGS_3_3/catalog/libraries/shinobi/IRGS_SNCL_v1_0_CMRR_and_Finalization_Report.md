## Source status and migration
The public source document is complete and portable. Inclusion does **not** adopt content, activate historical mechanics, invent a missing jutsu conversion or migrate any campaign. Only previously eligible selected content remains available under its old rules. Any conversion of a held technique requires its own full effect contract, test evidence and adoption decision. No historical jutsu ranks/values are transferred automatically.
