# IRGS Shared Content Library — Maturation R1

**Status:** Proposed qualification drafts, not operational promotions or replacement of source-backed holds.
**Active Core:** `IRGS-CORE-3.2`; **Catalogue:** `IRGS-SCL-1.0` with immutable `@0.2` families.

## Scope

This supplement deepens **36 previously held content families** in five neutral domains (materials, coverage, network nodes, shields and body plans). No source values or numerical adapters were replaced. The other **192 held families** have a companion remediation register. Thus **51 selected qualitative entries remain selected and 228 holds remain holds** pending an explicit per-definition release and acceptance.

These are content design proposals, not claims of live playtesting, numerical calibration, cross-engine implementation or source deprecation.

## Proposed qualitative contracts

### Head — `SCL:PBC-WPN-FL-COV-01@0.2`

**Source:** FL:COV-01  
**Family type:** coverage | **Current gate:** `source_backed_hold` | **Draft:** `qualification_draft_not_adopted`

**Purpose And Method:** Anatomy-facing protection-region label: Head. Identifies nominal regions, not any physical protective object.

**Activation:** Bind the actual wearer/body plan and precise material component locations before using head as a coverage description.

**Limits And Failure:** Nominal regional coverage is not complete protection and cannot imply thickness, fit, armor value, orientation or adjacent regions.

**Acceptance:** A head coverage tag without a corresponding actual protective component grants no resistance or inventory. Any uncovered subregion remains uncovered.

**Maintenance:** No independent maintenance; physical attached protection is maintained by the item/component owner.

**Instance Binding:** Coverage is a geometry descriptor, never an asset or lot. Modifying coverage does not materialize armor.

**Overrides:** Anatomies lacking the named region must map explicitly or mark the tag not applicable; no forced humanoid hit table.

**Runtime:** Resolve region intersection against current position/anatomy and real component coverage before consulting genre protection mechanics.

**Portability:** Applicable to any genre with compatible anatomy, protective equipment and contact geometry.

**Acceptance requirement:** Validate a real positive/negative fixture with the selected native adapter and publish a new versioned family record before replacing the hold.

**Family type:** shield | **Current gate:** `source_backed_hold` | **Draft:** `qualification_draft_not_adopted`

**Purpose And Method:** Physical protective equipment family: Buckler; source-specified typical purpose: weapon-control, close defense. It requires real size/material/configuration and is not a free defense action.

**Activation:** Bind one existing shield asset, grip/strap or deployment support, actor capability, orientation, protected sector, condition and selected genre protection/stress adapter.

**Limits And Failure:** No universal shield structure, coverage percentage, defense modifier, deflection or free reactive attack is implied. A shield behind the wearer does not protect the front.

**Acceptance:** One buckler appears as one owned asset; changing its defense use/deployed mode cannot create a second shield or protection outside its actual geometry.

**Maintenance:** Stress and repairs update the actually struck or defective component under the selected adapter; the mode choice does not renew condition.

**Instance Binding:** K-ASSET owns the individual shield and condition; K-ACTOR owns available action/commitment; stock only records separate physical lots.

**Overrides:** A setting may alter construction and authorization with explicit physical/profile evidence; no inherited Fantasy d100 modifiers in another engine.

**Runtime:** Check physical feasibility, hands/readiness and shield-bearing arc; apply only one appropriate protection/stress mechanism.

**Portability:** Shield family is reusable in any context with a physical shield and compatible protection adapter.

**Acceptance requirement:** Validate a real positive/negative fixture with the selected native adapter and publish a new versioned family record before replacing the hold.

**Portability:** Reusable in historical, survival, fantasy and technical contexts where this material exists, without imposing any era or fantasy physics.
