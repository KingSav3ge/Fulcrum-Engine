## Merge and reconcile

- **Retain:** Core 3.2 mechanics, SCL v1.0 identities, all selected contracts, all aliases and source payloads.
- **Combine:** R1 and R2 data in a source-pinned non-normative overlay and readable consolidated compendium.
- **Differentiate:** source facts, proposed operational interpretations, native-adapter calibration, authored case descriptions, bounded synthetic verification and actual runtime test execution.
- **Defer:** promotion and new immutable revisions until their per-family acceptance requirements are met.
- **Account removals:** no functional, procedural, source or inventory removals; superseded packaging READMEs and manifests are replaced as release metadata only. Original clean release manifest is preserved under provenance.

## Positive and negative regression classes

1. Identity, revision, alias, selector and source field remain valid; invalid target/selector is rejected.
2. All held targets have exactly one proposal; selected families are not inadvertently reclassified.
3. An authored contract never creates an asset, lot, action, authority, capability or new campaign history.
4. Source material in transit or reserved is not double counted as delivered or consumed.
5. A facility label or equipment entry does not grant operating capacity before configuration, commissioning, staff and resources exist.
6. Failed or Mixed work does not refund consumed stock or invent unrelated failure events.
7. Observation, transmission and receipt remain separate from actual physical effect.
8. A failed check does not generate a missing service, weapon, medicine, species property or technical parameter.
9. Qualified routine behavior does not require invented repeated dice; runtime only advances a defined event once.
10. Historical source package remains recoverable but is not a fallback active Core.

## Executed verification and limits

The package includes three reproducible check scripts. The full-package script validates integrity, immutable source anchors, catalogue and proposal coverage, status and compatibility, plus explicit invalid-reference predicates. The inherited R1 and R2 scripts validate additional structural fields and bounded synthetic causal/accounting examples. These **are not** the 420 per-entry scenarios executed in a complete IRGS runtime; no technical or historical claims are retroactively calibrated. The signed result is recorded in `RELEASE_VERIFICATION_RESULTS.json` after build.

Maintain archival v3.1 provenance separate from the active release.
