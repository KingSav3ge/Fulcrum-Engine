# ChatGPT continuity — comparison, reconciliation and release record

**Request:** The user reports losing information when recapping a day or chapter because play lacks checkpoints. **Date:** 17 October 2026. **Result:** shared ChatGPT Play Continuity Adapter 1.0 and complete AST 0.5 receiving documents, with Core 3.2 maintenance R3/SCL 1.0 unchanged.

## Diagnosis

The baseline already requires persistent state, recoverable compact journals, retained uncertainty, actor knowledge, exact event identity and a single simulation runtime. Its Core §§86/90/91 and AST E7 are sufficient owners for these functions. Adding a second “memory ledger” would duplicate responsibility.

The deployment gap is procedural. The play chat has no firm visible capture schedule or complete day/chapter-history procedure. A latest-state panel does not preserve a conversation that produced no numerical change. A brief retrospective recap cannot reliably recover details that were never recorded or are no longer accessible. Successive condensed summaries can omit distinct exchanges while still appearing coherent.

No actual gameplay transcript or failed day recap was supplied for this revision. This is a source-based diagnosis of the documented procedure and the user's reported failure, not a claim to have reconstructed missing events or measured an exact context limit.

The default five-reply interval is editorial and user-adjustable. It does not select a biological rate, combat duration or new campaign-number profile. A critical change or transfer can checkpoint sooner. Multiple triggers in one reply coalesce, and a long scene remains one scene across its checkpoint segments.

## What the new procedure preserves

Each turn record captures the declared/resolved action, participants, attributed dialogue meaning, actual changes, promises and the next unanswered choice. Ordinary interaction is eligible even without a mechanical change. Important exact wording retains its source; other wording is an attributed paraphrase. A source locator without accessible payload is not a substitute for the content it identifies.

The scene record accumulates all segments. A checkpoint exports the actual current frontier and its supporting coverage. A day/chapter chronicle is built from available scene/source records rather than unrecorded recollection. Every covered scene must have substantive account text. Missing source intervals prevent an unqualified complete-history claim.

The output is transparent about storage. A confirmed file save advances the durable frontier; a failed write or absent tool does not. Visible chat records still help, but this specification cannot guarantee that every earlier message will remain accessible indefinitely. Public checkpoints cannot reveal hidden state as a workaround for missing private storage.

## Ownership and agency checks

Turn notes, scene records, checkpoints and recaps are views/exports of the existing event/evidence history. They create no stock, time, injury, training, reputation meter or appointment. A promise, quote, accepted task and actual payment remain distinct. A recap request does not make the PC sleep, answer a question, spend money or travel to close a scene.

Causal time and record order stay separate. Simultaneous activity shares the existing pre-event rule; a report has its own later receipt without duplicating the incident. Replay and correction keep stable campaign/event identity. Earlier snapshots cannot overwrite later valid events. Open choices, retained results, fractions and commitments survive export and scale changes.

## Migration, calibration and unresolved work

Begin recording prospectively from the current supported frontier when the play chat selects this edition. Make the first checkpoint identify available state/history and earlier source gaps. Recover older history from actual transcript, scene files or user-provided corrections if needed; do not backfill plausible events and label them contemporaneous records. This build does not load, alter or reconstruct current campaign state.

Outstanding evaluation: whether five replies is comfortable in actual play, note size, retention of low-stakes social detail, retrieval effort, portable-export usability and success of a second runner continuing from the checkpoint. A live trial should compare source transcript to chronicle, counting omitted unique exchanges, false facts, wrong attribution, duplicated events, lost open choices and divergent recovered state. Existing combat/physiology/economy calibration gaps remain unchanged.
