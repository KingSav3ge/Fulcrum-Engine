### Coopian shield: accepted design draft

- Curved heater shield enlarged to suit Ian’s size.
- Large opposing relief cuts inspired by the supplied historical shield forms.
- Deeper relief on the sword side; shallower opposing relief.
- Substantial protection retained above and below both cuts.
- **Exactly five horizontal steel bands across the front.**
- **Heraldry painted directly onto the steel bands**, maintaining the intended design across the face.
- **Two traditional shield arm straps plus one smaller strap connecting to the over-bracer harness; quick-release clasp as corrected by S14.**
- **Handle on the sword side, beside the deeper relief.**
- Padding, bracer connections, fastening points and release arrangements consistent across main, detached and fitted studies.

“Sword side” is wearer-relative. Do not substitute screen-left/right or infer handedness from mirrored views. Experts in the game may identify changes through construction, fitting and testing. Record each proposed/accepted revision separately from actual work. The removable leather on the old shield is not the Coopian shield’s directly painted heraldry.
