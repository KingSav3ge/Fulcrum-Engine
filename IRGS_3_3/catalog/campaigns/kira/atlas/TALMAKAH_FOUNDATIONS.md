# TalmaKah: homelands and the desert crossing

TalmaKah, also recorded as TalmahKah, is a land of comparatively tribal and nomadic societies. The continent’s more beastlike peoples possess a greater degree of sentience than comparable peoples elsewhere in Kira. Their communities, conflicts and choices belong to the political life of the setting.

## The Lupine desert

The central desert is the Lupine homeland. Nomadic groups, traders and travellers cross it to reach the surrounding regions. These movements expose them to Lupine attacks. Lupine also raid out into neighbouring homelands and retreat into the desert, where their familiarity with the country makes pursuit difficult.

Scrap Haven, Urgustan, LackNa, Razor Tusk Ranch and Turtle Rock occupy this central cultural landscape, alongside the Woshi, Foxti, Nekhamen, Coyota and Dingishi communities. These named places anchor the map without defining the Lupine as one centralized kingdom or treating every gathering place as a permanent city.

The creator has established both the attacks on travellers and the raids beyond the desert. These are deliberate activities within a sentient society. The map supplies no individual tribal responsibility for a particular raid, so none of the five named communities is assigned a specific attack here.

## The power of familiar ground

The following develops the consequences of those foundations. A desert crossing creates exposure over time: a caravan has to sustain its people, animals and goods, while a raiding party can choose when to approach and when to withdraw. Knowledge of terrain, water and shelter can matter as much as the number of fighters. A force capable of defending a town can still struggle to follow raiders into country its scouts do not understand.

For neighbouring communities, the immediate threat and the cost of retaliation are different problems. Escorts can protect a particular crossing without ending the raids. A successful defence at the edge of the desert need not produce a safe pursuit. Traders and nomads can disagree about routes, protection and acceptable losses because their livelihoods expose them to different risks.

That makes the Lupine important to the continent’s relationships without requiring them to rule every road or occupy every border settlement. Desert mobility gives them a practical advantage; a particular raid or pursuit still depends on circumstances. Specific routes, watering places, agreements, seasonal movements and historical campaigns remain to be developed.

## Mapping conventions

The creator’s seven supplied colours identify the surrounding homelands. The Lupine centre retains a natural sand-and-ink treatment because no eighth colour was supplied. Labels and settlement markers identify its inhabitants. The atlas is an illustrated interpretation of the Minecraft reference, with no measured world-distance scale or new executable travel rules.

The supplied sheet contains only the Lupine entry: a height range of 5–6 feet, child weights of 20–80 pounds and adult weights of 80–140 pounds. Those are reference descriptions; the sheet supplies no further cultural history. The creator’s current statements establish the homeland and raiding relationship.
