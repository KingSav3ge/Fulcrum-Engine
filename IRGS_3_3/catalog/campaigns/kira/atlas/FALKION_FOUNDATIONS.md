# Falkion: island and maritime realm

The creator tentatively recalls an Atlantis-like concept with hostile sea elves, then selects **a fortified island and maritime realm** as the direction to develop. The atlas consequently depicts a living surface-island civilization and its surrounding waters. A drowned civilization or underwater capital is not selected.

The reference shows a small green main island in the eastern part of a predominantly oceanic map, with a few western islets and a cyan hand-drawn enclosure. The atlas interprets that line as a maritime perimeter for this concept. Exact unnamed harbour, sea-wall and watchtower positions are visual proposals, rather than recovered source locations.

Atlantis influences the maritime architectural aesthetic. The map supplies no literal Atlantis history, named ruler, divine patron, destruction date, newly named capital or predetermined war. Territorial hostility belongs to the proposed Falkion society; no universal attitude is assigned to every Sea Elf in Kira.

Falkion occupies its own continental shelf without needing to be classified as a continent. Equal atlas-page dimensions do not establish equal land area among regions.
