# Aldain: peoples and homelands

Aldain is dominated by lizard-folk adapted to their respective regions. The exception is the Primatarus Shenyan Republic, whose inhabitants are sentient monkey-folk inspired by Sun Wukong.

The source places the Scalian realm in the southwestern green lowlands, Rolling Hills in western snow country, Mountain Peak in the central-southern highlands, Eversong Hollow across the broad central/northern snowy mainland, Frozen Lake on the northern snowy island or near-island, and the Republic on the wooded eastern peninsula.

Regional adaptation is established, but exact physiological traits remain to be developed. Cold-country lizard-folk should not be removed from the map merely because familiar reptiles are associated with warm climates. The Republic’s monkey-folk inspiration does not insert the literal Sun Wukong as a ruler or make every resident a divine being.

The atlas develops settlement and architectural imagery around those foundations. Particular structures and local details are visual elaborations. Named rulers, exact constitutions, diplomatic history and a measured world-distance scale remain further work.
