# Azaloth: physical geography

The creator has not decided its peoples, cultures or nations. The atlas therefore develops physical geography only. This is an open design question, not a declaration that Azaloth is uninhabited.

The Minecraft reference supplies a northwestern lake, a larger northeastern waterbody, open northern and central green country, extensive western and southern woodland, central and southern river bends, east-central water, sandy coastal areas and eastern snow/high ground. The atlas naturalizes the pixelated terrain without assigning named settlements, states, ruins or rulers.

No shared world-distance scale or relative position among Kira’s six shelves is selected by the illustrated plate.
