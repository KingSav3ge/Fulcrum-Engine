# Bendara: physical geography

The creator has not decided its peoples, cultures or nations. Its atlas is physical geography only; it supplies no claim that the continent is empty or uninhabited.

The Minecraft reference emphasizes a large northwestern waterbody, open northern country, dense central and southern forest, scattered ponds, eastern sandy country cut by a winding river, and limited southwestern snow/high ground. Surrounding snowy Minecraft terrain outside the map panel and screenshot symbols are not imported as continent features.

Specific names for natural features, settlements, political borders and a measured distance scale remain to be developed.
