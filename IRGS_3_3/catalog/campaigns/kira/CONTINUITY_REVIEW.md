# Kira: current canon decisions

The expanded lore is the adopted setting canon under KIRA-U06. The original documents are a superseded creative archive. These decisions resolve inherited inconsistencies; the remaining topics are further worldbuilding work.

## Established decisions

### Kieran’s longevity

Kieran is a Plains Elf, also called Flatland Elf, born in 1000 B.A. and aged 2342 in 1342 A.A. His sustained healing practice, the Long Renewal, extends his life beyond the ordinary Plains Elven reference span of about 1000 years. It provides no immunity or guaranteed resurrection.

### Duration of the dwarven war

The continental dwarven war runs from 80 to 400 A.A., a span of 320 years. The 228-year figure describes Kieran’s active wartime service, not the duration of the whole war. Exact enlistment and withdrawal years are not separately fixed.

### The rebellion and separation dates

Estru and Kyrinus gain independence in 500 A.A. Estru’s later failed invasion of Sadion belongs to 600 A.A.

### Spelling and identity variants

Use Akarian for Akaria’s dwarves; Evalian for Everwood’s political identity; Sadire for Sadion’s human people; Estrusian for Estru’s civic identity, including its human and Desert Elven populations; Karthinian for Kyrinus’s civic elven society; Daniel Convor for Dan/Fox. Plains and Flatland denote the same elven group; Tree and Forest denote the same elven group. Karthi remains a distinct historical name.

### Map capitals and written place names

Karkaria is Akaria’s underground capital; Karwathis is its superseded map label. Evalia names the republican capital as well as the republic; Evalian on the map is a label variant. Ikarion is the current place name; Ikrarion is an older spelling for the same place.

### Early Sadion chronology

The early sequence is Sadire migration and settlement, Grad’s consolidation and founding of the Legion, followed by San’s institutional development. Sadion is unified by the opening of trade with the elves in 1998 B.A. The ambiguous archival phrase After 2000 supplies no separate date or 2000-year interval.

## Further development

Estru’s precise constitution, some jurisdictions and office-holders, exact Ice and Desert Elven caste positions, world distances, relative shelf positions, detailed magic rules and selectable starting snapshots remain to be developed. The western voyage’s destination and outcomes remain open stories. None requires agreement with an older document.

The original map transcription retains its duplicate marker 25 and uncertain small lettering. Those are limits of the image reference, rather than a veto on naming and developing the places.
