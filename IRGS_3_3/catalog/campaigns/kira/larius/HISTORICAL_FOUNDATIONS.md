# Larius: the adopted historical framework

The expanded worldbook supplies the current historical reference. The original national histories are superseded creative material. Their contradictions no longer constrain the setting.

| Period | Current canon |
| --- | --- |
| Around 3000 B.A. | Barvaris Akarian unifies the dwarven clans. |
| Around 2500 B.A. | Sadire migration and the major elven disease crisis. |
| By 1998 B.A. | Sadion has consolidated; human–elven trade opens. Grad precedes San in the kingdom’s early development. |
| About 1010–1000 B.A. | Evalian civil war and the republican founding. Kieran is born in 1000 B.A. |
| Ascension | Dwarves emerge onto the snowy surface; the calendar turns. |
| 40 A.A. | The foundation of Kyrinus as an Evalian colony. |
| 80–400 A.A. | The continental dwarven war, lasting 320 years. Kieran’s active service spans 228 years within it. |
| 110 A.A. | Dwarven war machines bring the colonies close to defeat. |
| 200 A.A. | The Evalian–Sadire alliance begins recovering occupied lands. |
| 400 A.A. | Peace settlement. |
| 500 A.A. | Rebellion, independence of Estru and Kyrinus, Kieran’s mixed refugee rescue and the founding of the Rangers. |
| 600 A.A. | Estru’s failed invasion of Sadion; Kyrinus declines to join. |
| 1342 A.A. | Reference present. Kieran is 2342; Cathius is 75. |

Desert Elves were initially unwilling participants in the history of Sadire settlement and became foundational contributors to Estru’s culture across the centuries. Exact settlement dates and the original coercive arrangements remain undeveloped. Ice Elven isolation is shaped by unsuitable habitat across much of Larius and dwarven dominion over the suitable region; no dated expulsion or conquest is established.

## Canon authority and creative history

Creator statements KIRA-U03 through KIRA-U05 establish the national inspirations and elven relationships. KIRA-U06 adopts the expanded lore as working canon. The Long Renewal, eight religious traditions, supporting cast and other expanded material belong to this edition.

The original nine documents and exact transcription remain available through the [archive](../archive/README.md). Their quotations document the world’s creative history. Later authoring can revise inherited details without preserving a conflicting old claim as an unresolved fact.
