The supplied audit identified useful patch components and material gaps. The current delta repairs discovery/entry selection, protects feature discovery, corrects opening information discipline, captures optional structured testimony and introduces non-executing historical assessment. It does not convert the patch's unresolved first turn into actual gameplay consequences.

Full Stage-One completion remains open: general action adjudication, grounded autonomous NPC reactions, automatic narration, calendar advancement, durable epoch summaries, full service contracts and realized family/skills Life-Path initialization are not implemented. No full historical campaign playtest or complete visual/browser pass is claimed.
